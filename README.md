## Alpha

SideKick is in **alpha**. It is used every day and improves quickly, but expect rough edges, and things can change between versions. Updates arrive on their own (see [Updates](#updates)).

## What it does

**Player**
- Its own player, up to 1080p60, about **0.6 s behind live**, and it catches up by itself when it falls behind
- Rewind a live stream, with timeline pictures and how far behind live you are
- Recordings remember where you stopped, with a chat that follows the video
- Clips: watch, download and create them. Volume is separate from the stream
- Floating mini player, audio-only mode, theatre mode, Shazam on any stream or clip
- GPU or CPU video decoding, your choice

**Chat**
- [7TV](https://7tv.app) emotes, combos, flying emotes, paste pictures, GIFs from Giphy and Tenor
- Mentions list, highlight words, friends, pinned message, polls, predictions
- Subs, gifts, raids and KICKs shown as proper events
- Chatters badge with who is writing right now
- Pop-out chat and **SideChat** (better Chatterino), a chat window for streamers
- Moderator tools and a moderator dashboard

**Watching more**
- **Multistream**: up to four streams at once, all counting as watched (channel points, drops and the daily reward)
- Follows synced with your Kick account, a live sidebar, go-live notifications, favourites in your own order
- **Big Picture**: a keyboard-driven mode for the couch or a TV (Ctrl+Shift+B), off by default
- Themes (Kick and GTA VI), English and Czech, tray icon, keyboard shortcuts

## Install

1. Download `SideKick_<version>_Setup.exe` from the [latest release](https://github.com/AnakUn69/sidekick-releases/releases/latest).
2. Run it. Windows may show a blue *"Windows protected your PC"* screen, because the installer is not signed yet. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Pick your options (desktop shortcut, automatic updates) and press **Install**.
4. Open SideKick and press **Sign in**. A small window asks you to log in to Kick.

It installs for your user only, so no administrator rights are needed. Windows 10 and 11 are supported.

## Updates

SideKick checks this page for new versions and installs them by itself, but never in the middle of a stream. Change it any time in **Settings → Updates**: *Automatic*, *Ask me* or *Off*. The same page lists every version with what changed. Every installer is signed, and the app checks the signature before it installs anything.

## Uninstall

Windows **Settings → Apps → SideKick**, or run the installer again and choose **Uninstall**. You can also remove your saved settings there.
